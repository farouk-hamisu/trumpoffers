Overview

Trump Offers provides rewards, recruitment and digital membership cards. This policy explains what we collect, why we use it, how we protect it and the choices you have.

By creating an account, contacting us or applying for a role, you agree to this policy and our Terms & Conditions.

Security reminder: We never ask for card PINs, card security codes or online-banking passwords. Card numbers are entered only through our encrypted card form and are seen only by authorised staff.

What we collect

We only collect what we need to run the platform and your membership. Depending on how you use it, we collect:

  • Account details — your name, email address, phone number, general location and secure login credentials.
  • Reward records — reward amounts, dates, balances and transaction history.
  • Withdrawal details — withdrawal amounts, cardholder name, masked card details and payment status. Card numbers are encrypted. We never collect card PINs, card security codes or banking passwords.
  • Job applications — your background, qualifications, work history and uploaded resume.
  • Digital card details — chosen design, cardholder name, card reference and verification status.
  • Support messages — the content of messages you send us and your contact details.
  • Technical logs — session identifiers, IP addresses and browser details used to keep the platform secure.

How we use it

We use your information to run and protect the platform, including to:

  • Create your account and keep access secure.
  • Add rewards and maintain your balance and transaction history.
  • Process withdrawal requests and keep accurate records.
  • Review job applications and arrange interviews.
  • Issue and verify digital membership cards.
  • Reply to your support messages.
  • Prevent fraud and security threats.
  • Keep the platform reliable and auditable.

Trump Offers does not sell personal information or share member profiles with third-party advertising networks.

Lawful bases

We process your data where we need it to provide your account and features, where we have a legitimate interest in keeping the platform secure, where the law requires it, or where you have given consent.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run the platform, such as hosting, sign-in, email delivery and file storage. They must protect your data and may not use it for their own purposes.

We may disclose information where a court order, law enforcement request or law requires it. We never sell member data or share it with advertising networks.

Storage & retention

Member data is stored securely with access controls and encryption. Resumes and candidate documents are kept separately and are available only to authorised staff.

We keep account data while your membership is active. Recruitment materials are kept for up to 24 months after a decision unless you ask us to delete them earlier. Support message logs are kept for up to 36 months.

Security

We use encryption in transit, secure password hashing, role-based access and audit logs. Only authorised staff can access administrative tools.

If you notice suspicious activity on your account, contact security@trumpoffers.com immediately.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, or ask us to restrict how we use it.

You can update your profile in the member dashboard. For a formal privacy request, email privacy@trumpoffers.com from your registered email address.

Cookies & local storage

We use necessary session cookies and local storage to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

Our optional live-chat widget is provided by Brevo. Loading it connects your browser to Brevo and may store chat preferences. Messages you send through live chat are processed by Brevo on our behalf. You can block third-party scripts to disable the widget; the rest of the platform will still work.

Children

Trump Offers is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes

We may update this policy as the platform changes. Material changes will appear on this page with a new effective date.
