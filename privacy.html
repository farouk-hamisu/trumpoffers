Overview

Trump Offers is a demonstration experience for rewards discovery, career applications and digital card design. We take privacy seriously even though the balances, claims, withdrawals and card details on this platform are simulated. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, where it is stored and the control you retain.

By creating an account, submitting a message or applying for a role, you agree to the practices described here and in our Terms & Conditions.

Please note: Trump Offers never needs, and will never ask for, real banking credentials, card PINs, CVVs or full payment card numbers. Do not enter them anywhere on this platform.

What we collect

We keep collection narrow and tied to a clear purpose. Depending on how you use the platform, we may collect:

  • Account details — your name, email address, telephone number, location and a password managed by our authentication provider.
  • Reward activity — simulated claim references, claim status, selected reward, and the timestamps associated with each step.
  • Withdrawal records — simulated amount, method, a destination label and an account name you provide. We never ask for real account numbers, PINs or CVVs.
  • Recruitment information — your application answers, qualifications, experience, location and an uploaded resume.
  • Card studio information — the design you select, the cardholder name you choose and the fictional card reference generated for you.
  • Messages — the content of enquiries you send to the concierge desk, with your contact details and chosen subject.
  • Technical data — minimal device and browser information needed to keep the service secure and working, plus local storage used for the preview environment.

How we use it

We use personal information to operate the demonstration faithfully and to respond to you. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • Record simulated reward claims, processing windows and unique references.
  • Record simulated withdrawal requests so you can review their status.
  • Review job applications, track their progress and communicate outcomes.
  • Generate and verify fictional digital card designs on request.
  • Respond to concierge enquiries and support requests.
  • Protect the platform against abuse, fraud and unauthorised access.
  • Understand aggregate usage so we can improve the experience.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Lawful bases

Where data protection law applies, we rely on the following bases: performance of a contract when we provide your account and requested features; consent when you ask us to respond to an enquiry or when you opt in to communications; legitimate interests in keeping the platform secure and improving it; and legal obligation where records must be retained.

Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run the platform, and only to the extent needed. These include our authentication and database provider, our hosting and content delivery provider, and our email delivery provider. Each is bound by contractual confidentiality and security obligations.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights and safety of Trump Offers, our members or the public. We do not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Storage & retention

Account, reward, withdrawal, application, card and message records are stored in a managed PostgreSQL database with row-level security. Resumes are stored in a private storage bucket and are accessible only to authorised reviewers through time-limited links. Site media is stored in a public bucket because it is intended for display.

We retain account records for as long as your account is active. Recruitment records are retained for up to 24 months after a decision, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Message records are retained for up to 36 months. Preview-mode data stored in your browser remains until you clear it or reset the environment.

Security

We use encrypted transport, hashed passwords, role-based access control and database row-level security. Administrative access is limited to named administrators and is logged. Uploaded files are validated for type and size and stored privately.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at security@trumpoffers.com. Because this is a demonstration platform, we encourage you to use a unique password and never reuse a password you use for real financial services.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate details, deletion of your account and associated records, restriction of processing, portability of the data you provided, and withdrawal of consent where processing relies on it.

You can update your name, phone and location from your dashboard. To exercise any other right, write to privacy@trumpoffers.com from the email address associated with your account. We respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority.

Cookies & local storage

We use a small number of essential cookies and browser storage keys to keep you signed in and remember interface preferences. We do not use advertising cookies. You can clear local storage through your browser settings; doing so will sign you out and reset interface preferences.

Children

The platform is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. Material changes will be announced on this page with a revised effective date, and where appropriate by email. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to privacy@trumpoffers.com, or by post to Trump Offers, One Trump Place, Suite 1200, New York, NY 10004. You can also use the concierge desk and select “Account access”.
