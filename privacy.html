Overview

Trump Offers provides an executive rewards, recruitment, and digital membership card platform. We maintain rigorous privacy and security safeguards for all personal data entrusted to us. This Privacy Policy details what information we collect, the specific purposes for which it is processed, where it is secured, and the choices you retain over your information.

By registering for an account, submitting an enquiry, or applying for an executive vacancy, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy and our Terms & Conditions.

Security reminder: Trump Offers never requires or solicits card PINs, CVV codes, or online-banking passwords. Payout card numbers are accepted only through the encrypted card-connect form and are shown only to authorised payout administrators.

What we collect

We restrict data collection to information necessary for platform functionality and verified membership services. Depending on your interactions with the platform, we collect:

  • Account profile — full name, email address, contact phone number, general location, and secure credentials managed by our identity provider.
  • Reward activities — claim references, verification timestamps, settlement progression, and reward account records.
  • Disbursement details — withdrawal amounts, selected transfer channels, beneficiary names, encrypted bank or crypto details, and masked destination labels for settlement tracking. Payout card numbers are encrypted at rest; CVV, PIN and online-banking credentials are never collected.
  • Recruitment applications — professional background, qualifications, work history, and uploaded curriculum vitae/resumes.
  • Digital card credentials — selected finish surface, cardholder embossing name, issued 16-digit reference number, and verification status.
  • Concierge correspondence — message content, subjects, and contact details transmitted via the concierge support desk.
  • Technical audit logs — secure session identifiers, IP addresses, browser agents, and cryptographic verification logs.

How we use it

We process personal data strictly to administer our services, maintain security, and deliver an elevated member experience. Specifically, information is used to:

  • Authenticate your identity, provision member accounts, and maintain secure access.
  • Process reward claims, manage settlement workflows, and track status.
  • Administer withdrawal requests and maintain accurate financial ledgering.
  • Review candidate applications, manage candidate communications, and schedule interviews.
  • Issue, personalize, and verify authentic digital membership credentials.
  • Respond promptly to concierge support enquiries.
  • Detect and mitigate security threats, fraudulent behavior, and policy breaches.
  • Maintain operational performance and administrative auditability.

Trump Offers does not sell personal information or share member profiles with third-party advertising networks.

Lawful bases

We process personal data under established legal standards, including: performance of contract to provide your membership account and requested platform features; legitimate interests to maintain operational integrity, protect against fraud, and uphold platform security; compliance with legal obligations; and explicit consent where specifically provided.

Sharing

We disclose personal information exclusively to contracted service providers who facilitate essential infrastructure operations, including cloud hosting, secure authentication, transactional email delivery, and private file storage. Every service provider is bound by strict non-disclosure and data protection agreements.

We may also disclose information where mandated by court order, law enforcement inquiry, or applicable legal process. We never distribute member directories to commercial data brokers.

Storage & retention

Member data is stored in enterprise PostgreSQL databases protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) and encryption in transit and at rest. Resumes and candidate documents are housed in isolated, access-restricted storage buckets accessible only to vetted talent officers.

Account data is retained for the active lifecycle of your membership. Recruitment materials are preserved for up to 24 months post-decision unless earlier deletion is requested. Operational message logs are maintained for up to 36 months.

Security

We enforce modern cryptographic standards, including TLS 1.3 transport encryption, salted password hashing, role-based access controls, and comprehensive database audit logs. Administrative access is restricted to verified personnel and monitored through secure administrative dashboards.

If you suspect suspicious activity relating to your account, report it immediately to security@trumpoffers.com.

Your rights

Under applicable privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA), members may request access to their personal data, correction of inaccuracies, complete data deletion, processing restrictions, or portable copies of their records.

Profile information can be updated directly within your Member Dashboard. To submit a formal privacy request, email privacy@trumpoffers.com from your verified account email address.

Cookies & local storage

We utilize strictly necessary session cookies and local storage tokens to maintain authenticated sessions and interface preferences. We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking beacons.

Our optional live-chat widget is provided by Brevo (Sendinblue). Loading the widget connects your browser to Brevo’s Conversations service and may store chat preferences in your browser. Messages you choose to send through live chat are processed by Brevo on our behalf. You can disable the widget by blocking third-party scripts; the rest of the platform continues to work normally.

Children

Trump Offers is an executive platform designed exclusively for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly solicit or maintain data from minors.

Changes

We periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect platform improvements and regulatory developments. Any material changes will be announced on this page with a revised effective date.
