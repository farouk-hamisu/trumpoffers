07 / Questions

A little clarity.

Everything you might reasonably want to know before creating an account. If something is still unclear, write to us.

Ask a question

Upon creating your verified member account, a welcome reward balance of $30,000 is allocated to your profile. You can initiate reward claims and submit withdrawal requests via your preferred method directly through your member portal.

You move through a structured four-stage workflow: review, confirmation, verification, and completion. A unique reference code is generated for your audit trail, with live status updates delivered straight to your dashboard.

The digital membership card serves as your official credential across the Trump Offers ecosystem. Each card carries your unique member ID, expiration date, and bespoke design finish, verifiable on our public verification portal.

Job applications are acknowledged immediately with a unique reference. The estimated review period is three to four business days. You can follow progress at any time from the application tracking page using your reference and email address.

Members can submit withdrawal requests to their chosen destination (bank transfer, digital wallet, or card credit) through the secure withdrawal desk. Each request is reviewed by our operations desk with clear status tracking at every step.

Never. Trump Offers will never ask for online banking passwords, PINs, CVVs, one-time codes or full card numbers. If anything on the platform requests those details, do not provide them and contact us immediately.

Your account data is stored in Supabase with row-level security, so you can only access your own records. Resumes and application data are visible only to authorised administrators. Read the full privacy policy for details and your rights.
