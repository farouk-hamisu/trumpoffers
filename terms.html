Rewards & claims

Verified member accounts receive an initial reward allocation of $30,000, viewable directly within the member dashboard. Initiating a reward claim generates a unique tracking reference (e.g. TO-RW) and enters the verification queue. Claims progress through multi-step administrative verification and processing stages.

Claims are subject to verification against fraudulent activity, account duplication, or terms violations. All disbursements and redemptions comply with platform settlement schedules.

Withdrawals & disbursements

Members may submit withdrawal requests for available reward balances exceeding the minimum threshold of $100. Members designate an approved destination method (Bank Transfer, Digital Wallet, or Card Credit) and provide the destination account details.

Withdrawal requests are processed through operations desk review. While a request is in review or processing, the requested amount is held in reserve. Completed requests are settled according to the estimated timetable of the chosen disbursement channel.

Digital cards

The Trump Card Studio provides digital membership credentials featuring custom finishes, unique 16-digit card references, validity periods, and cardholder embossing. Cards submitted through the studio undergo credential verification and administrator review. Approved cards can be verified publicly using their reference code and last four digits.
